#### notesForNextWaves
API: MeshLibrary.LowKey(key) returns '<key>_low' only when that key is usable on this device and its manifest LayoutSig equals the High key's (both absent also counts as equal); the result is memoised and cleared by _Reset. MeshLibrary.SetLod(target, 'High'|'Low'|'Hide') is client-only and returns false on undressed targets and ML_Weld targets. 'Low' rebuilds ML_Mesh from the low key and puts ML_LodKey on the mesh. Parts Dress already hid (Transparency 1) are never marked.
